## Campus & clinical resources for Georgia Tech students

- **Georgia Tech Office of Disability Services (formerly ADAPTS):**
  - Location: Smithgall Student Services Building (Suite 220), 353 Ferst Dr NW, Atlanta, GA 30332
  - Website: [disabilityservices.gatech.edu](https://disabilityservices.gatech.edu/) • Phone: (404) 894-2563
  - Services: Academic & dining accommodations, housing meal plan exemptions, and assistive technology.

- **Georgia Tech Dining Services & Campus Dietitian:**
  - Website: [dining.gatech.edu](https://dining.gatech.edu/) • Email: `dining@gatech.edu`
  - Services: 1-on-1 dietary consultations, specialized meal ordering, and allergen management across Brittain, North Ave, and West Village.

- **Stamps Health Services (Georgia Tech Primary & Allergy Care):**
  - Location: 740 Ferst Dr NW, Atlanta, GA 30332
  - Website: [health.gatech.edu](https://health.gatech.edu/) • Phone: (404) 894-1420
  - Services: Clinical consultations, allergy and gastrointestinal referrals, and medical accommodation letters.

- **Klemis Kitchen (Food Assistance Initiative):**
  - Website: [studentlife.gatech.edu/klemis-kitchen](https://studentlife.gatech.edu/klemis-kitchen)
  - Services: Discrete food pantry access for Georgia Tech students facing food insecurity or special dietary access barriers.

- **Swiss Interest Group Histamine Intolerance (SIGHI):**
  - Food Compatibility List: [histaminintoleranz.ch](https://www.histaminintoleranz.ch/en/introduction.html)
  - Recognized standard classification of dietary histamine content, DAO blockers, and histamine liberators.

- **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 AAA):**
  - W3C Web Accessibility Initiative: [w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)
