Dining halls are built for rapid high volume, not for tracking food age or histamine levels. These guidelines help Georgia Tech students navigate campus meal plans safely.

## Georgia Tech Dining Hall Station Strategies

### 1. Brittain Dining Hall (East Campus / Bobby Dodd Way)
- **Grill Station:** Ask the grill cook for a plain grilled chicken breast or burger patty seasoned only with salt and olive oil. Request it fresh off the grill rather than taking one from the warming tray.
- **Salad Bar:** Select whole raw vegetables (cucumbers, carrots, radishes) and hard-boiled eggs. Avoid premade pasta salads, olives, pickles, and dressings that contain red wine/balsamic vinegar. Use plain olive oil and salt.
- **Timing Tip:** Visit at 11:00 AM for lunch or 5:00 PM for dinner right when fresh batches are brought out from the kitchen.

### 2. North Ave Dining Hall (Freshman Hill / North Ave Apartments)
- **Rotisserie & Carving:** Plain roasted meats are safer if carved directly. Avoid pre-sauced barbecue, teriyaki, or smoked cuts.
- **Hot Entrees:** Steam-table dishes that sit under heat lamps accumulate histamine quickly. Prioritize steamed jasmine or brown rice, plain roasted potatoes, and steamed green vegetables over casseroles or slow-simmered stews.
- **Avoid Cross-Contact:** High-traffic pizza, pasta, and deli stations have frequent tomato sauce, parmesan, and cured meat cross-contact.

### 3. West Village Dining (West Campus)
- **Made-to-Order Bowls & Stir-Fry:** Take advantage of customizable bowl lines. You can ask cooks to omit soy sauce, teriyaki, tofu, and fermented dressings, opting for simple garlic, oil, and salt instead.
- **Breakfast / Brunch:** Plain scrambled or fried eggs with boiled/baked potatoes. Skip bacon, sausage, and aged cheeses.

---

## Questions to ask dining staff

- *"When was this dish cooked? Was it prepared fresh today, or reheated from a previous shift?"*
- *"Does this contain tomato paste/sauce, vinegar, aged cheese, cured meat, or soy sauce?"*
- *"Could you grill a plain chicken breast or burger patty for me with just salt and olive oil?"*
- *"Is there a kitchen manager or dining dietitian on duty who can verify recipe ingredient lists?"*

## How to request official Georgia Tech accommodations

1. **Office of Disability Services (formerly ADAPTS):**
   - Located in the **Smithgall Student Services Building (Suite 220)**.
   - Website: [disabilityservices.gatech.edu](https://disabilityservices.gatech.edu/)
   - Phone: (404) 894-2563
   - Schedule an intake appointment and provide medical documentation from a physician or allergist detailing functional digestive/dietary limitations.

2. **Tech Dining Registered Dietitian & Management:**
   - Tech Dining employs a dedicated campus dietitian who meets 1-on-1 with students requiring dietary accommodations.
   - Contact: [dining.gatech.edu](https://dining.gatech.edu/) or email `dining@gatech.edu`.
   - Accommodations can include pre-ordering allergen-safe meals, chef-prepared custom plates at Brittain/North Ave, or special swipe exemptions if medically necessary.

3. **Klemis Kitchen (Food Assistance Initiative):**
   - Serving Georgia Tech students with unique dietary needs or food insecurity: [studentlife.gatech.edu/klemis-kitchen](https://studentlife.gatech.edu/klemis-kitchen).
