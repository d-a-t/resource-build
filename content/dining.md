## Questions to ask dining staff

- "When was this dish cooked? Was it made fresh today, or is it from a previous day?"
- "Does this contain tomato, vinegar, cured meat, aged cheese, or fermented ingredients?"
- "Can you cook something to order, such as plain grilled chicken and rice with olive oil and salt?"
- "Is there a dietitian or manager who can talk with me about my needs?"

## What to look for

- Made-to-order stations (grill, omelet, stir-fry) over steam-table dishes that sit for hours.
- Whole, simple foods: plain rice, baked potatoes, fresh-cut vegetables, fresh fruit.
- Times when food is freshly put out, such as the start of a meal period.
- Sauces, dressings, and soups, which often contain vinegar, tomato, or store-bought broth. Ask for oil and salt.
