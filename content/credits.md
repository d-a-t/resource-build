## Credits & Acknowledgments

This toolkit was developed as a student-centered digital health and accessibility project for the Georgia Tech community, combining low-histamine nutritional guidance, live campus dining evaluations, and universal design principles.

---

### AI Usage

AI has been used in assitance with this project, specficially **Gemini** for guiding the site to conform with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AAA** and **ADA Title II / Section 508**, and creating the scraper to pull food data from Tech Dining Nutrislice API.

### Data Sources, Clinical Research & Standards

- **[Swiss Interest Group Histamine Intolerance (SIGHI)](https://www.histaminintoleranz.ch/)**
  - Clinical research publications and food compatibility reference data for evaluating histamine content, DAO inhibitors, and histamine liberators.
- **[W3C Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/)**
  - Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and 2.2 specifications, techniques, and evaluation standards.
- **Georgia Tech Campus Resources**
  - The [Office of Disability Services](https://disabilityservices.gatech.edu/) (Smithgall 220), [Stamps Health Services](https://health.gatech.edu/), and [Klemis Kitchen](https://studentlife.gatech.edu/klemis-kitchen) for campus dining accommodation guidelines and food security frameworks.
